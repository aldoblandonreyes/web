About Me

At A&F New Era Painting LLC, we are passionate about transforming spaces with high-quality painting services. With over 16 years of experience in the industry, we specialize in creating spaces that not only stand out but also last over time. Our team is made up of highly trained professionals who use top-notch materials and the most innovative techniques to ensure flawless finishes and long-lasting results.

What sets us apart:

-Exceptional quality: We ensure that every project, big or small, is treated with the same dedication and commitment to quality.
-Attention to detail: Every corner matters. We focus on the small details that make a difference and add a unique touch to your space.
-Customized solutions: Whether you’re looking to refresh the look of your home or enhance the image of your business, we work closely with you to bring your vision to life.
-Environmental commitment: We use eco-friendly products whenever possible, ensuring that our work is not only beautiful but also sustainable.
We offer a wide range of services, from residential painting to commercial painting, tailoring our work to meet the needs of each client and ensuring the project is delivered on time and within budget.
